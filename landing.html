Why a graph

Learn in the order the skills actually depend on each other.

Exam guides give you a list of topics. This gives you the connections between them: what to learn first, what it unlocks next, and where the certifications overlap.

Top-down

Start from a certification goal and see every sub-skill it requires, weighted by the exam guide.

Bottom-up

Mark what you already know and the graph shows which skills unlock next.

Shortest path

Pick any skill and get the minimal roadmap to it from where you are today.

Gamified

XP per skill, day streaks and badges. Progress stays private in your browser.

Certification tracks

Choose where you're headed.

Each track opens the graph filtered to the skills its exam tests. A skill shared by several certifications is one node, so progress carries across tracks.

Coverage

Across the Databricks platform.

Skills are grouped into product areas, from Delta Lake and Unity Catalog to Vector Search and AI/BI Genie.

How it works

From “where do I start?” to exam-ready.

  1. Pick a trackFilter the graph to one certification, or view every track at once.
  2. Place yourselfAnswer short practice questions on a skill to mark it mastered and earn its XP.
  3. Follow the pathUnlocked skills light up. Ask for the shortest path to any skill and work through it.
  4. Go deeperEvery node links to its Databricks Academy course and the relevant docs.
